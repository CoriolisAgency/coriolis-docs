These docs answer operator tasks: domain, payments, shipping, FFL Cockpit, POS sync, and GunSearchEngine usage. Support handles the cases the docs cannot see, which is your live store.

## What to send

Open a ticket from the address on the account. Include:

- Store domain
- What you expected and what happened
- Order number, SKU, or distributor, if it is a single record
- Screenshot of the WooCommerce or Cockpit screen, not a phone photo of the monitor
- Whether this started after a change you made

Do not send card numbers, full gateway secrets, or a customer's license image in the first message. We will ask if we need a credential, and we will tell you where to put it.

## What we will not do in a ticket

We do not give legal advice, ATF opinions, or bound-book instructions. Transfer law and your electronic bound book stay with your compliance vendor and your counsel. We also do not rebuild a theme inside a same-day ticket. That is a scoped change.

## When to use the docs instead

Search this site before you write. Catalog import, ship-to-FFL settings, and AIM sync steps are here so you are not waiting on us for a click-path. If the doc is wrong for your store, say so in the ticket and name the page. That is how the manual gets corrected.
