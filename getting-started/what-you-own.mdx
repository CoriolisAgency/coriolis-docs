You own the WooCommerce site, the domain, and the customer list. Coriolis hosts the install, configures FFL Cockpit and the shipping rules at setup, and supports the stack. If you leave, the site export goes with you. The theme and the catalog are not locked to a proprietary storefront.

## What is yours

- The domain and the DNS zone. You point it. We send the records.
- WooCommerce pages, products, and order history on your install.
- The customer accounts and the email list you collected on that domain.
- Policy language. We do not invent your transfer policy.

## What Coriolis runs

- Hosting on WP Engine, backups, and the SSL certificate after the domain is attached.
- Kadence as the theme base, with the edits a dealer is allowed to make written down in [theme edits](/woocommerce/theme-edits).
- FFL Cockpit and FFL Checkout when the plan includes them. Configured at setup. The Cockpit license stays with the plugin vendor.
- The payment gateway plugin, after you send credentials for a processor that allows firearm sales.

Plan details live on the [ecommerce page](https://www.coriolisagency.com/ecommerce). This page is ownership, not a price list.
