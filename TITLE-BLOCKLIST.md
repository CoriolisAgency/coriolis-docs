# Title blocklist

Reject the page in review if the `title`, H1, or meta description contains any of these, including close variants:

- FFL website pricing
- FFL website plans
- gun store website plans
- FFL Accelerator
- ffl accelerator
- AmmoReady alternative
- Gearfire alternative
- FirearmCart alternative
- Gunpowdr alternative
- Orchid alternative
- best FFL ecommerce
- can you use WooCommerce to sell guns
- WooCommerce vs Shopify
- RetailBI alternative
- switch off RetailBI
- 4473 automation

Also reject:

- A stand-alone bound-book or Form 4473 procedure
- An H1 that names a plan price
- Any link to `fflaccelerator.com` or `gunsearchagent.com`
- A second Demand Intelligence marketing page

Procedural phrases are fine: “migrate off AmmoReady checklist” as a task, linking up to `/ammoready-alternative`, is allowed. The comparison H1 is not.
