These are the operator guides for a store Coriolis hosts. They cover the clicks: domain, payments, ship-to-FFL, FFL Cockpit, AIM sync, and the GunSearchEngine script. They are not the plan list and not a comparison page.

Start with [what you own](/getting-started/what-you-own), then the [launch checklist](/getting-started/launch-checklist) if the domain is not live yet.

## What lives here

- **WooCommerce.** Your storefront. Kadence is the theme. You own the site and the domain.
- **FFL Cockpit.** Catalogs, MAP, and FFL Checkout on that store. The plugin vendor keeps the license. Coriolis configures it on the managed stack.
- **POS sync.** AIM first. The register and the site stay in step. The sync does not file a transfer form or replace your bound book.
- **GunSearchEngine.** What shoppers typed on your site. Install and observe mode are here. The product itself lives on GunSearchEngine.

See [how the Coriolis stack fits](https://www.coriolisagency.com/stack) on coriolisagency.com.

## How to use a page

Each guide answers the task in the first paragraph, then gives the click path. If a step does not match your install, open a ticket and name the page. That is how this manual gets corrected. Do not paste gateway secrets or a customer's license into a doc comment.
