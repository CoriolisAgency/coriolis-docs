---
title: "What you can edit in Kadence"
description: "Which Kadence and WordPress edits are safe on a Coriolis store, and which ones to leave for support."
---

Kadence is the theme on a Coriolis store. You can change copy, images, and menus. You cannot restyle checkout or remove the FFL selector and expect orders to survive. Structural edits go through support so a plugin update does not wipe them.

## Safe to edit

- Page copy on Home, About, and policy pages, in the block editor.
- The menu items under Appearance, then Menus. Do not delete Cart or Checkout.
- The logo and the footer phone number, in the Customizer.
- Product photos and short descriptions. Those are product fields, not theme files.

## Leave alone

- Checkout blocks and the FFL Checkout template. A block you remove is how the dealer selector disappears.
- Plugin files, the child theme's `functions.php`, and any snippet you found on a blog.
- WooCommerce status page tools that rewrite permalinks. Permalinks are set. Changing them 404s the catalog.
- A second page builder. Kadence is the builder. Another one fights it.

If you want a layout change, send the page URL and a note of what should move. We scope it. Same-day tickets do not include a theme rebuild.

What is included at setup is on the [ecommerce page](https://www.coriolisagency.com/ecommerce).
